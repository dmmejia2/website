About Me

I teach, research, and build programs at UTEP—with students and colleagues across the borderlands.

I am Assistant Professor of Instruction in Computer Science at UTEP. I direct the M.S. in Software Engineering program and serve as Education, Workforce & Professional Development Lead for the Institute for Applied AI Innovation (AAII). Since July 2025 I have been CAHSI Lead for the GenAI in CS Education Consortium, coordinating generative-AI curriculum efforts across CAHSI institutions.

I teach core CS courses from first-year computing through our software engineering practicum—including topics such as data structures, agile software development, and professional orientation for majors. My research spans agile-centered software engineering education (including an active PI-led study of professional competencies in CS 4381, CS 5381, and CS 5389), generative AI in the classroom (including an ongoing UTEP PI study with M. Frias and N. Villanueva-Rosales), and earlier smart-city and knowledge-graph scholarship grounded in my graduate work. I am co-PI on NSF S-STEM (about $4.98M, Sep 2022–Aug 2028, with S. Salamah and M. Martin) and was PI on Google exploreCSR ($24K) and TensorFlow ($6K) awards.

From 2020 to 2024 I was a Visiting Assistant Professor at UTEP, where I developed the Applied Agile Software Engineering curriculum, served as Google Faculty in Residence and Academic Lead for UTEP/Google Tech Exchange, and co-led the UTEP/VISA Financial Literacy course team. Recent publications include SIGCSE and ITiCSE 2025 on generative AI in software engineering; an ITiCSE 2026 poster is accepted. I am a triple UTEP alumnus (B.S. cum laude, M.S., Ph.D.).

I coordinate 50+ student instructional employees each semester as TA Coordinator, support graduate-program operations (including Fast Track) as Graduate Student Coordinator, advise the Coding Interview Club, and serve as CodePath institutional liaison—alongside committee work from software-course leadership and scheduling to the university Tech Enhanced Learning advisory board. I care most about inclusive, industry-connected computing education at an HSI on the U.S.–Mexico border.
